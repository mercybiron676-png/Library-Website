About the Library

Our library is a welcoming space where students and the wider community can read, learn and grow. We bring together a wide collection of books, digital resources and quiet study areas.

Our Mission

To give everyone free and easy access to knowledge, and to support learning, research and a love of reading.

What We Offer

A growing collection of textbooks, novels and reference materials, computers with internet access, and friendly staff ready to help you find what you need.
